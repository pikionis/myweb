# pikioniswebdeveloper — personal site of Petros Pikionis

Astro 5 + GSAP (ScrollTrigger). Static, multi-page site: Home `/`, Services `/services`, Projects `/projects`,
About me `/about`, Contact `/contact`, Blog `/blog`. In three languages: English at `/`, Greek at `/el/`, German at `/de/`. Site text lives in `src/text/*.json`; projects and posts are Markdown in `src/content/`. Petros edits all of it in the admin panel at `/admin`.

## Commands
- `npm install` → `npm run dev` (http://localhost:4321; `PUBLIC_LANG=el npm run dev` for the Greek site, at /el/) → `npm run build` (every language, in `dist/`, `dist/el/`, `dist/de/`; `scripts/build.mjs`)
- `node scripts/check-words.mjs` lists text on the pages that isn't in `src/text` (it would stay in English on the other languages' pages)
- Live at https://onehublabs.com: GitHub Pages, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` on every push to `main` and daily (fresh GitHub terrain). The domain is registered at Hostinger; its DNS points at GitHub Pages.
- A push deploys in about two minutes and keeps the PageSpeed results already live (`dist/pagespeed.json`); the daily run measures afresh, and so does `gh workflow run deploy.yml` (run it after fixing a client site), about ten minutes.
- **The panel commits straight to `main`.** Pull before working: `git pull --rebase origin main`.

## Structure
- `src/text/*.json` — every word of the site, as `{ en, el, de }` (`site`, `stories`, `services-page`, `my-story`). English stands in for a translation that is missing. A new word goes here, in all three languages, never into a component
- `src/data/site.ts` — types and code around `src/text/site.json`, which holds: text, services (keys `web apps commerce search`: lead / body / chips, claim / summary for the /services card, `includes` = everything it covers, in full), `bots` (AI services & bots: a fifth service in llms.txt only, not on the home page, which needs real projects; on /services its chat and automation are part of AI SaaS & Mobile Apps), `cardOrder` (the llms.txt order), stats (shown on /about), UI words (`ui`), page-head defaults (`meta`). `words` holds what sits around the content (footer, forms, page titles). `need` on a service is the key of an option in `brief.needs`
- `src/data/lang.ts` — the languages. Each is a build of its own: `lang` is this build's (`PUBLIC_LANG`, set by `scripts/build.mjs`), `t()` picks a `Copy`, `to('/services')` is an address in this language (every internal link goes through it), `unlocalized()` and `inLang()` turn a page's address into another language's, `day()` a date, `upper()` capitals on a canvas (Greek capitals lose their accents; CSS does this itself, given `<html lang>`). `localize` in `astro.config.mjs` keeps only the build's own language of each word when it reads `src/text`, so a page or script never carries the others'. Under `/el/` and `/de/` only the built pages and `_astro/` exist; everything in `public/` is at the root, once, and `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` (with every language), `llms.txt` (English) and `/admin` exist only at the root. Base.astro declares the language (`<html lang>`, og:locale, hreflang for every page). Space Grotesk has no Greek letters: Manrope's (`public/fonts/manrope-greek.woff2`) stand beside it, fetched only by a page that has any. German is Swiss spelling (ss, never ß; «quotes»; 800’000), formal Sie, KI for AI. `src/components/LangSwitch.astro` is the switch, in the top bar and the footer's legal row
- `src/data/servicespage.ts` + `src/text/services-page.json` — every word of /services in `Copy`: hero, picker, the four sections (title, short name, promise, bullets, chips, proof, timeline, `weeks` for the timelines, `startingFrom` = null hides the price, cta), how we work (each step names its story picture and its crop), the timelines, the FAQ (12 pairs, with chip names), the form (`budgets` empty = the budget field hidden), the words in the drawings. Proof cards name real projects; `url: null` hides the link
- `src/content/projects/*.md` — one file per project, and each has a page (`/projects/<id>`). `el/<id>.md` and `de/<id>.md` beside them hold only its words in that language (title when its name is written differently there, summary, blurb, badge, tags, metric, scope, the body as its story; links in them carry the language: `/el/about`), merged by `getProjects()`. `services:` lists the keys it shows under (none = only on /projects); `url` adds "Visit site"; `live_url` is the live site in its frame (home card, /projects) and what PageSpeed measures; `launching: true` = not live yet ("Launching soon", no link, frame or measurements); a live project without `live_url` (FeedGenix and OurBnb: their sites refuse frames) shows its `cover` instead of a frame, on its card and on /projects, and is not measured; `stack` = what it is built with; `cover` = a tall capture of the site (800px wide WebP in `public/projects/`); `blurb` its own text on the service sheet; `badge` a label; `metric` + `scope` an engineering card instead of a picture (DeinDeal). The body is its story. Copy `_template.md`.
- `src/content/blog/*.md` — one file per post; `draft: true` shows only in `npm run dev`. Copy `_template.md`. `el/<id>.md`, `de/<id>.md` are its translations (`getPosts()` in `src/data/posts.ts`).
- `src/layouts/Site.astro` — every page: the logo's shapes (LogoDefs), Nav, the page, the Contact block as footer, the intro overlay, and `init()` from main.ts
- `src/data/logo.ts` — the onehub logo as path data (generated by `scripts/logo-parts.py` from the brand folder; don't edit by hand). Colours are the `--ink / --cobalt / --ivory` tokens
- `src/components/LogoDefs.astro` defines the shapes once; `Logo.astro` is the header lockup, top left: symbol + wordmark (the link home) and `brand.motto` under the wordmark; it draws them with `<use>`. The name is not in the header (it captions the portrait in the hero and on /about)
- `src/components/LogoIntro.astro` + `src/scripts/intro.ts` — the opening intro ("One Continuous Line"). The head script in `Base.astro` picks the mode before first paint (`html[data-intro]`: full once per session on the home page, still with reduced motion, quick = header logo fade, which is what every other page gets, so a visitor arriving there gets the page at once) and has a 3s failsafe; `main.ts` waits for `intro.handoff` before the ink title and the entrances. It plays on the hero's own dark ground (#0a0b0a) in the header's colours there (ivory, the arc cobalt), so the logo lands on itself and the page it opens onto is the same ground (an ivory intro made PageSpeed count every second of it as an empty screen). It is measured once Space Grotesk 500 is in (its line wraps differently in the stand-in face; preloaded on the home page; a second at most) and after the page's first picture (`painted()`), and set up in three tasks (reads, the first frame, the timeline). Strings live in `intro` / `brand` in site.ts
- `src/components/` — Nav (split menu + logo), Intro (curtain + hero), Marquee, Services (stacked sheets), Terrain (GitHub, on /about), Contact
- `src/scripts/main.ts` — all motion: curtain, letter/word reveal, light sheet (home only), hero bubbles, section entrances (`data-rise`, faded by opacity only, so a heading waiting to rise is still in the page's outline; what rises can't have a CSS transition on opacity, or GSAP reads a value on the way and rests there). `init()` starts the intro, then sets everything else up in turns (`later`: one part per task, starting after the first picture, so no part holds a phone or the first paint); the services stack is set up a moment after the intro hands over, or as soon as the page nears it (`whenNear`); the bubbles read their colour once and move only once the hero shows. Lab numbers: see `scripts/pagespeed.mjs` below
- `src/scripts/splitnav.ts` — the menu (port of KokonutUI Split Nav, MIT): split on the current page, hover gaps, scrub/flick, arrow keys. Top right on desktop; on a phone (≤820px) it floats at the bottom of the screen (the island and the /services cards keep clear of it)
- `src/components/BriefForm.astro` + `src/scripts/brief.ts` — the idea/brief form on /contact (files by drop or picker, 10 MB total). Sending lives only in `post()` (brief.ts), which both forms use (this one and the closing form on /services): for now FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint (`brief` in site.ts); swap it for our own backend later. Never submit either from tests: intercept `formsubmit.co` — a real send emails Petros.
- `src/scripts/assistant.ts` — the brief assistant's interface (questions → final brief, both streamed). Only `previewAssistant` exists: templates, `npm run dev` only, never shipped. The real one (an endpoint streaming from the model, loaded with the projects and services) implements the same interface in the backend phase.
- `src/components/Services.astro` + `src/scripts/workstack.ts` — home "What I build": service sheets stack over a pinned screen and each sheet's projects stack inside it (one scroll step per sheet/project; `--steps` must match the timeline). It pins on screens ≥861px wide and ≥740px tall, and only if every sheet's words fit beside its projects (measured when the stack is set up, a moment after the intro or as the page nears it, and on resize). On a phone held upright (≤860px wide, ≥560px tall, motion allowed) it holds its own way (`holdPhone`, class `.phone`): the sheets stack like tabs under the bar (each sheet sits `--tab` lower than the one before, so every earlier title band stays showing); its title band stays while its words scroll up under it one-for-one with the finger, then its projects stack as on a desktop, each with its own text (`.ptext`: the blurb, or the summary while the blurb is a [TODO], and the story link) under its picture, then the next sheet slides up over it. A covered sheet's band shows "See again" (`data-again`), which scrolls back to the moment that sheet was in. On both desktop and phone the sheets behind step back as each new one comes in (`recede`: a little smaller and darker per level via scale and `--shade` on `.svc::after`), like folders inside a folder. On phones the list of project texts before the cards is hidden. The stack's height is set from the measured words (built once the fonts are in, rebuilt when the width changes). Elsewhere (a short wide screen whose words don't fit, a phone on its side, reduced motion, no JS) it is the plain flow, so no copy is ever cut. Each sheet shows its projects' `blurb` (the summary while it is a [TODO]: a placeholder never reaches the page, nor does a story's `cta.note`) beside the projects, crossfading with the card on top or under the pointer. AI search, marketing & ads shows `proof` (this site) as its card. Project videos lazy-load and play only in view.
- Each service sheet has a background slot (`background` in site.ts: a `--sheet-*` fill token or an image under a 4.5:1 scrim) and a "Read more" button that opens its story
- `src/data/stories.ts` + `src/text/stories.json` + `src/components/Stories.astro` + `src/scripts/stories.ts` — the service stories (`#story-web`, `#story-saas`, `#story-ecommerce`, `#story-ai-search`). A sheet's "Read more — how I work for you" opens its story as a "thought": small rounds rise from the button and a rounded box grows over the home page, titled with the service; inside, the story plays by itself (`storyPlayer`): each scene's picture draws itself, its words light up, it holds by its word count, then the next; bars, prev/pause/next, a tap on the scene, ←/→ and Space take over, and it pauses while the tab is hidden. After the last scene the pictures FLIP into a grid, all at once, with the CTA and "Play again" (wide box: a storyboard sized by container units so both rows and the CTA fit without scrolling). Reduced motion: it opens straight on the grid with every scene's words. Close, Esc or a click beside the box shrinks it back into the button. The page never moves: while open it is `inert` and wheel/touch are stopped unless the grid itself scrolls (its overflow is left alone, which keeps its layout still); the article is `tabindex=-1` so arriving on its address keeps focus in it. Rendered after `<main>` in index.astro, in the HTML from the start (without script the stories simply follow the services). The CTA goes to `/contact?type=project&service=…`, which pre-selects the brief form's need (`need` in site.ts)

A result is the median of five runs: Google's machines differ a lot from run to run (a page's Speed Index 0.8 s, then 3.9 s), and the deploy log has a line per run with what it saw (`seen`, the parts of the LCP, the machine; its round trip reads 0 whatever the distance, so don't judge by it). Two causes it has shown. Google's machine now and then keeps its screen blank for a second and a half after the page has loaded (the main thread idle, the film blank), which only Speed Index follows: that is why /projects neither shows nor counts Speed Index. And the LCP estimate counts every request at High or Medium priority that ends before the picture, so fonts and scripts landing just before it doubled it (La Isla's 1.6 s and 3.5 s, Kalimera's 1.1 s and 4.3 s): on those home pages the fonts now start after the first picture and the scripts ask at low priority. Distance moved only the sites without Hostinger's CDN, and not enough to cost points (Delian's first paint 0.2 s asked from Greece, 0.9 s from the US runner). Judge a change by this, not by a local Lighthouse: Google's machines are slower and have no graphics chip, so what runs on the graphics chip here runs on their processor there (the WebGL ink cost the home page a TBT of 420 ms there and none here). Whatever it can't measure keeps its saved results; `-- --lhr <dir>` reads Lighthouse reports instead (how the results are made while the keyless API is out of quota: Lighthouse with devtools throttling and `--lang=en-US`, PageSpeed's own locale, the median of three runs per device). /projects shows a project's results only while all of them are green (`green()` in ProjectsHub)

## Voice (his brief)
- **Greek: calm, warm, friendly and professional, in sound Greek syntax. It must never read as translated.** Greek word order («έναν πολύ αγαπημένο οικογενειακό φίλο», not «φίλο της οικογένειας»), whole sentences with verbs rather than chains of nouns and colons, `εσύ`, the reader never assumed to be a man. English only where Greeks say it too (e-shop, marketplace, feed, SEO, Google Ads, AI, startup, accelerator): «ιστοσελίδα», not site; «πες μου την ιδέα σου», not pitch; «περιγραφή», not brief; «σχόλια», not feedback; «πρώτη έκδοση» in running text, not MVP. A business that writes its own name in Greek is written that way on the Greek pages, in the right case (Αρωγή, της Αρωγής; `title` in `el/<id>.md`, and `name` on a /services proof card).
- **German: strictly professional.** Swiss spelling (ss, «quotes», 800’000), formal Sie, KI for AI, no jokes, colloquialisms or exclamation marks. Software is «entwickelt», never «gebaut»; always Marktplatz, Unternehmen, Online-Shop, Start-up, Offerte, innert, Lancierung; generic masculine, no paired forms.
- The English is his and stays as it is; its jokes ("Hold my beer") exist only there. A rewrite keeps every fact and number. Words in drawings have little room: check them on the page.

## Rules
