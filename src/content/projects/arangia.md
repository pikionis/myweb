## Three apartments, one small destination

Arangia is three apartments. Its site makes them feel like a small boutique destination.

It does not look like a rental site. It reads like a travel magazine, or the site of a small boutique hotel: editorial, minimal and warm, with a Mediterranean soul.

## One tree

The whole identity rests on a single symbol: the bitter orange tree. The name comes from it, *nerantzi* in Greek, *arancia amara* in Italian, and everything returns to it: the texts, the calendar, the rhythm of the pages.

## Slow living, no rush

The mood is slow and quiet, with a nostalgia for the Mediterranean. The site does not try to sell; it invites. Its message is simple: slow living, without the rush.

## Behind the site

Behind the brand runs the booking operation, across Airbnb and Booking.com. And the site speaks four languages: English, Greek, German and Italian.
