## A logo was enough

Ilias, the owner of Arogi, a learning-support centre in Kos, came to me to build its website. His digital suitcase held one thing: a logo.

For me, that was enough.

## One idea, the whole site

That logo became the idea behind everything. The concept of the site, its illustrations, its graphics: all of it grew out of that one mark. It is one of the most beautiful designs I have made, and I made it for a very dear family friend. I am really proud of the result, and genuinely excited about it.

## Behind the pages

Behind the site sits a dashboard where around ten authors write for Arogi's blog, each on their own or together. And running the site is easy: the content management system behind it is simple to use.
