## Born at DeinDeal

FeedGenix was born while I was making 800,000+ products manageable at DeinDeal: from solving a problem for one company, to a product for many.

## From Feed Intelligence

Its first version, Feed Intelligence, took a dataset through six stages, routed the work across several AI providers and translated a 220,000-SKU catalogue. FeedGenix is built anew on what that proved.

## Connect, review, translate, publish

Connect a feed, by URL or as a file. The AI proposes where each column belongs and you approve it; when it is unsure, the field stays empty rather than wrong. Translate it into the languages you choose and publish one feed URL per language. Prices, stock, barcodes and SKUs never reach a model: they pass through exactly as they came.
