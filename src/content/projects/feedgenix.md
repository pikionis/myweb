## Born at DeinDeal

Feedgenix was born while I was making 800,000+ products manageable at DeinDeal: from solving a problem for one company, to a product for many.

## Upload, pick, translate

Upload a large dataset, pick the fields you want translated (the title, the description, the attributes) and the languages you need. Behind it, six stages share the work and route it across several AI providers; it has handled 220K SKUs.
