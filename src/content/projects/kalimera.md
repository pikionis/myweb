## Three sides of one trip

Kalimera brings together a travel guide, an online booking system and an affiliate network. The traveller gets better information, the local business a free booking system and new customers, and whoever brings users to the platform a reward for it.

## From Kos to Athens

I led the product design and shaped the business model. We launched the MVP in a local market: 80 sales in the first year.

In 2022 our pivot took 2nd place at Idea Platform, the contest in which CapsuleT, Greece's incubator for technology and tourism, filters business ideas. In 2023, as a team of six, we took part in the main CapsuleT competition, at its accelerator in Athens.
