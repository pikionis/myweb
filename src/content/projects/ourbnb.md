## From our own homes

OurBnb began with our own homes: for years we have welcomed guests at Arangia through Booking.com and Airbnb. Now it looks after other owners' homes on Kos, listed on Booking.com, Airbnb and Expedia: prices, guests, cleaning, maintenance and payments, for one fee.

## What the platform does

Every reservation comes in by iCal, and a change moves whatever depends on it. A check-out and the next check-in become a cleaning on the day's schedule, sent to the team on WhatsApp; the linen on each property's rack is counted and restocked, and a cleaner can report a fault on the spot. Guests get their check-in guide and answers on WhatsApp, from an AI assistant that answers from stored data and passes whatever it is unsure of to a person.

Each booking gets its statement and each owner a monthly payout, with a portal showing their bookings, earnings and documents. The stay declarations to AADE keep their deadlines.

## Built to be trusted

It never logs in to Airbnb or Booking.com and keeps nobody's passwords: it reads calendar feeds, files and messages. Guests' ID numbers and lockbox codes are encrypted and deleted on schedule. The rules live in the code: the AI turns free text into commands and never decides one.
