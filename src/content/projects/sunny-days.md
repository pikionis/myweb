## A family-run hotel in Tigaki

Sunny Days is a family-run hotel on the beach of Tigaki, on Kos: thirty rooms, two pools and a pool bar, run by the same family from the very beginning.

## By day, by night

The site opens on the hotel by day; one switch shows it by night.

## Three languages

It speaks English, Greek and German, and "Book a stay" is always one click away.
